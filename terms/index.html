Premium and purchases

Premium is available for the first seven days without a charge. The trial does not automatically make a purchase. Basic SSH stays free after the trial.

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase, not a recurring subscription. The purchase screen shows the price in your App Store region before you confirm. Apple handles payment and purchase approval.

You can cancel before confirming a purchase in Apple's purchase sheet. There is no recurring tmoxie subscription to cancel. Use Restore Purchases in the app to restore an eligible purchase with the same Apple Account.

For a refund request or billing issue, use Apple's billing and purchase support. Apple determines refund eligibility.

Your remote environment

Connect only to machines and accounts you are authorized to use. You control the remote commands and actions you send. Closing sessions or panes, managing worktrees, and running agents can change files or stop processes on your machine.

SSH access requires a reachable host. tmux, Herdr, coding agents, VPNs, and other services may have their own installation requirements, charges, and terms. A tmoxie purchase does not include those services.
