Privacy Policy

Effective: August 8, 2026

The short version: tmoxie has no developer-operated account system or application backend, and uses no ads, tracking, or third-party analytics. The app does not automatically send us your credentials, terminal contents, prompts, or usage data.

About this policy

tmoxie is an iOS SSH and tmux client provided by the tmoxie developer. This policy covers the tmoxie app and tmoxie.app. tmoxie keeps its operational app data on your device and does not automatically upload it to us. Data can leave the device when you send it to an SSH host, share it, open Web Preview resources, or use Apple platform services and backups under your device settings.

Data kept on your device

tmoxie stores the information needed to provide its features locally:

  • saved-host labels, hostnames or IP addresses, ports, and usernames;
  • SSH passwords in the iOS Keychain, with device-only, non-synchronizing Keychain protection;
  • trusted SSH host-key fingerprints;
  • settings, shortcut layouts, appearance choices, local connection counters and dates, remembered Web Preview addresses, and local trial or purchase markers;
  • Agent Inbox items, which can include an agent name, message, host, session, window and pane routing information, and timestamp; and
  • a diagnostic log, which can contain technical events and identifiers such as SSH username, hostname or IP address, session name, remote path, and error text.

The diagnostic log remains on your device and grows until you clear it from Settings, Diagnostics. Raw terminal byte recording is off by default, must be enabled for the current app launch, and stops at 4 MB. A raw recording can contain terminal input and output, including sensitive text. Neither file is sent to us automatically. You choose whether and where to share it with the iOS share sheet.

tmoxie does not provide app-level iCloud sync. Apple may include ordinary app-container data in device backups according to your Apple and device settings.

Connections and content you choose to send

tmoxie connects directly from your device to SSH hosts you specify. SSH traffic can include authentication data, terminal input and output, tmux session metadata, composer messages, clipboard text, and photos or files you select. SSH protects that traffic in transit. The destination host and software running there receive and process the content under their own configuration and policies; tmoxie does not proxy SSH traffic through a server operated by us.

When you attach a photo or file, tmoxie uploads it to ~/.tmoxie-uploads on the selected host. tmoxie does not automatically delete these remote files. They remain until you or the host removes them.

Web Preview tunnels a web service on the selected host through the existing SSH connection and uses a non-persistent WebKit data store. A page you open can itself request third-party resources or services. Those destinations may receive request information under their own privacy policies.

Permissions and Apple services

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: used only after you start dictation. tmoxie requires on-device recognition and disables dictation when on-device recognition is unavailable. Audio is not saved or sent to us.
  • Photos and Files: only items you select through system pickers are read for annotation or upload.
  • Clipboard: read only after an explicit paste or Clipboard Image action. Text pasted into a terminal and images you upload are sent to the selected SSH host.
  • Local Network: used to reach SSH hosts on your LAN or VPN.
  • Face ID or device authentication: used to unlock the app when App Lock is enabled. tmoxie receives only the authentication result, not biometric data.
  • Notifications and system activity surfaces: local agent alerts can contain agent messages and routing details and may appear outside the app according to your iOS notification settings.
  • StoreKit: Apple processes purchases, restores, and transaction status. tmoxie stores only local entitlement and trial state needed to provide access.

Apple may process purchase, notification, device-backup, and optional crash or diagnostic information under Apple’s privacy policy. tmoxie includes no built-in or third-party crash-reporting SDK and does not add its own analytics service.

Links that you choose to open can leave tmoxie for your browser or mail app. Apart from Apple platform services and resources requested by a Web Preview page, tmoxie’s terminal, prompt, and file transport goes only to the SSH host you select.

Support and information you voluntarily share

If you email support or share a screenshot, log, or diagnostic bundle, we receive the email address and content you send. We use it only to respond, diagnose problems, improve tmoxie, and prevent abuse. Please remove passwords, private keys, access tokens, and unrelated terminal content before sharing. We do not sell this information. You may ask us to delete a support message or attachment by emailing tmoxie.support@gmail.com.

Your controls and deletion

You can delete saved hosts, trusted host keys, inbox items, debug logs, and byte recordings in the app. Delete a saved host before uninstalling tmoxie to remove its stored SSH password through the app. iOS can preserve Keychain items across an uninstall, including local trial and purchase markers, and tmoxie has no remote access to those items. Remote attachments and terminal data must be deleted on the SSH host.

Website

tmoxie.app is a static site with no tmoxie analytics or advertising cookies. Its hosting provider may process standard request data, such as IP address, browser information, and request time, to deliver and secure the site.

Children

tmoxie is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as tmoxie changes and will revise the effective date on this page. Questions or privacy requests: tmoxie.support@gmail.com.
